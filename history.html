Our Legacy

1. The Beginning (1951): Maison Luxe began as a small bespoke tailoring shop on the Rue Saint-Honoré in Paris. Our founder, André Luxe, believed that perfect tailoring was an art form that could improve a person's life.

2. Post-War Elegance: During the mid-50s, the brand grew rapidly. André’s ability to merge post-war practicality with luxurious silks made him a favorite among European royalty and Hollywood elite.

3. The Second Generation: In 1978, the founder’s daughter took over the Maison. She introduced the concept of "ready-to-wear" luxury, making the brand more accessible without sacrificing quality.

4. Global Expansion: By the turn of the century, Maison Luxe had stores in New York, Tokyo, and London. The signature minimalist luxury look became synonymous with global success.

5. The Modern Vision (Today): Our current directive focus is on sustainability. We are proud that our new production facility is 100% solar-powered and all fabrics are certified ethical.
